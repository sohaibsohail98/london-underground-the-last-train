LAST TRAIN station kit (Phase F1)
=================================

Original in-house geometry. There is no CC0 modular station kit (see
docs/reference/asset-sources-phase-f.md), so the shell, platform edge, tunnel
portal, pillars and furniture are modelled here. Surfacing is CC0 ambientCG,
credited in ../Surfaces/SURFACE-SOURCES.txt.

Authored in-engine through NeoStack geometry scripting: box and cylinder
primitives combined with booleans, then welded, normals and UVs recomputed,
Nanite enabled and built. Every piece is one closed mesh with UV channel 0.

Module contract
---------------
Base grid          100 uu
Kit module         400 uu
Wall thickness     150 uu
Room height        360 uu
Tile line          220 uu (wainscot below, painted concrete above, proud trim)

Pivots. Wall, pillar, portal and stair pieces stand on Z=0 with the pivot
centred in X and Y, so an actor placed at a floor coordinate needs no Z offset.
The platform edge is the exception: its deck top is Z=0 and the fall runs to
Z=-130, so it is placed at the platform surface height.

Materials are assigned per actor as an OverrideMaterial, not baked into the
mesh: the geometry-scripted meshes carry no material slot of their own. This
matches how the Phase B greybox already drove MI_<Set> onto its cubes.

Legal. Original design language throughout. No roundel, no Johnston or New
Johnston, no operator livery. Panel proportions, mounting heights, tactile
paving patterns and tunnel ring spacing are factual station architecture and
are fair game per docs/reference/reference-frame-notes.md section 3.
