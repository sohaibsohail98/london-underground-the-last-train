Open bug: corridor stall on queued zombies

NeoStack item 2.6 reproduced the exact stall the fix was meant to kill, just moved one place back in the queue. With 6 zombies alive: the front one (BP_Zombie0) stayed in melee and attacked fine. BP_Zombie1 to 5 sat in a single-file line, each ~68 units apart, with velocity = (0,0,0), for 12.5+ continuous seconds. All had a valid non-null CurrentTarget and AttackCooldown = 0, and were 68 to 342 units behind the front zombie, well outside AttackRange = 130.

Diagnosis: DriveTowardsTarget()'s MoveToActor is not re-issuing, or is being starved, when a zombie's path is blocked by another pawn's capsule in a narrow corridor. My direct-push fallback only triggers on EPathFollowingRequestResult::Failed or a missing controller, not on "path technically valid but blocked by a pawn ahead". The queued zombies think they have a live move request and just wait.

Fix direction (for the fresh session): in ALTZombieCharacter::DriveTowardsTarget, also apply the direct AddMovementInput nudge when the zombie has a move request outstanding but its velocity has been near-zero for more than ~0.5s while still outside AttackRange. Track a small StallTimer. Possibly add a per-instance lateral offset to the target point so the crowd does not form a perfect single-file conga. Keep RVO at 45. This is a bounded C++ change, compile and re-verify.
