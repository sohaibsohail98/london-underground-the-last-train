# Free 3D and kits

Genuinely free, Unreal compatible modular station kits, prop packs and reference
models. This file COMPLEMENTS `docs/reference/free-assets.md`, which is the
project's vetted acquisition list. Where that file already covers something, it
is marked "in free-assets.md" and not re described. New finds are marked NEW.

## Already in free-assets.md (do not duplicate, cross reference only)

- **Subway Environment** (Epic, Fab, free, UE-Only Content). Modular platform,
  tiled walls, columns, ceiling, stairs, a detailed train, grunge decals, water
  and steam Niagara. NYC/Toronto styling, no real network IP. The single highest
  impact grab for this arena. See free-assets.md section A.1.
- **Downtown West Modular Pack**, **Downtown Alley pack**, **Stack O Bot**,
  **Content Examples** for ticket hall, wet ground grime, PCG tunnel lining,
  feature reference. free-assets.md section A.
- **City Sample Crowds** for undead Londoner variety. free-assets.md section B.
- **Game Animation Sample**, **Animation Starter Pack**, **Lyra** for animation
  and weapon FX wiring. free-assets.md section B and C.
- **Niagara Examples Pack** for combat FX. free-assets.md section C.
- **AmbientCG**, **Poly Haven** for CC0 PBR surfaces. free-assets.md section A.
- **Kenney** audio and blaster kit, **Sonniss** GDC bundle, **Freesound** for
  sound. free-assets.md section C.
- **Overpass / Public Sans / Barlow** OFL fonts. free-assets.md section E.

## Quixel Megascans

Free with an Epic account inside UE (Fab). Since 2025 only a rotating subset is
free, check the price flag per asset. Best for concrete, metal, debris and
grime surfaces and for rubble/prop scans. See free-assets.md caution.

## Repo discipline (from free-assets.md)

- **CC0 and MIT** assets and the project's own work may be committed.
- **CC-BY / CC-BY-SA** may be committed if the attribution and licence text are
  kept alongside (add to `Content/LastTrain/CREDITS` or the repo attribution
  file). CC-BY-SA is viral, prefer CC-BY or CC0 for anything that gets modified
  and shipped.
- **UE-Only Content and Fab Standard** (most Epic and Fab content): ship it
  compiled into the game, do NOT commit the raw `.uasset` / FBX / textures.
  These packs are gitignored and fetched per free-assets.md.
- **3D Warehouse / SketchUp** models: reference only, not shippable, not
  committed.
- Verify Mac / Apple Silicon support and 5.8 compatibility for anything with
  code or heavy Nanite/World Partition content.

## Gaps (no good open source found)

- No CC0 or clearly free deep-tube (curved profile) train model. The 3D
  Warehouse 1996 Stock is reference only. Build the hero train from the
  dimensions in `rolling-stock.md`, or retexture and reshape the S Stock CC-BY
  model.
- No confirmed free, confirmed clean (no roundel, no Johnston) London
  Underground signage decal pack. Build signage from ISO 7010 SVGs plus an OFL
  font per `signage-and-wayfinding.md`.
- No dedicated CC0 "Tube poster frame" or "cross track advertising band" kit.
  Model the frame extrusions in engine (see `advertising-and-dressing.md`).
- No CC0 London Underground bench model with the correct design language. Kenney
  and Poly Haven benches are generic; model the LU perch and slatted bench from
  the description in `advertising-and-dressing.md`.
