# Canary Wharf station research

Reference material for blocking out and later dressing the LAST TRAIN arena, a
fictionalised Canary Wharf Underground and Elizabeth line station. The grey box
layout it feeds is `docs/reference/canary-wharf-grid.md`. The art target is
`docs/reference/reference-frame.png`.

## The legal line, in brief

Real London station names and geography are factual and are used deliberately.
Everything that is Transport for London trade dress is off limits: the roundel,
the Johnston and New Johnston typeface, the official Tube map and line diagram,
Elizabeth line and Jubilee line official livery and colour stripe arrangements,
operator logos, official wayfinding artwork reproduced as is. This folder records
architecture, proportions, spatial layout, materials, mounting positions and
panel shapes freely, and only describes the trade dress in words. Copyrighted
photographs are linked and described, not embedded. Open licence assets that
permit redistribution may be downloaded and are logged in `manifest.md` with the
licence.

## Files

| File | What it covers |
|---|---|
| `station-architecture.md` | The two real stations: Jubilee line box (Foster, 1999) and Elizabeth line box under Crossrail Place (Foster and Arup, 2022). Dimensions, escalator banks, materials, columns, ceiling, the vertical stack, and which features suit the arena composition. |
| `materials-and-surfaces.md` | Practical surface list for a UE5 art pass: concrete finishes, metals, tile, tactile paving, floors, handrails, wear patterns, with CC0 PBR texture sources. |
| `signage-and-wayfinding.md` | Descriptive only. Underground wayfinding hierarchy, panel shapes and mounting heights, next train indicator placement, and free OFL transit style typefaces that are not Johnston. |
| `advertising-and-dressing.md` | Standard poster sizes and frame types with real dimensions, placement rhythm, and station clutter: litter, bins, benches, abandoned items, with open reference sources. |
| `free-3d-and-kits.md` | Genuinely free and open Unreal compatible modular station kits, prop packs and models, cross referenced with `docs/reference/free-assets.md`. |
| `rolling-stock.md` | The tube train itself: 1996 Stock and Class 345 dimensions, deep tube body profile, doors, interior, cab end, exterior detail for the train as a wall view, and where a real train shows wear. Open 3D references. |
| `manifest.md` | Every file in the folder and every external URL referenced, grouped by category, each with source, licence, and whether anything was downloaded. |
